## Question

After the `target_mod_13 == 4` classifier pocket became support-starved, the
next question is whether the post-discovery q286 hole closes because tail
pressure disappears, or because tail pressure persists but the complement
rescues it.


complement exceeds the negative first-three contribution.

## Interpretation

This is the cleanest current answer to the “loop tightening” question.  In
these finite q286 blocks, the later hole does close: not because active/tail
stress disappears, but because the complement contribution exceeds the amount
needed to cancel it on every checked post-discovery tail row.

That does not prove an infinite theorem.  The discovery block is an explicit
obstruction to any theorem that claims unconditional rescue over all checked
blocks.  A real proof route must define an arithmetic condition, range, or
signed-correlation cone that explains why the complement lower bound fails in
the early stress block but holds in the post-discovery blocks.

The next theorem target should be:

```text
complement_to_principal_ratio
  > -first_three_modes_to_principal_ratio
```

on a non-circular q286 target class, or an averaged/signed cone estimate that
forces the same positive rescue margin.
